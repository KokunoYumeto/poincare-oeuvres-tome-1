# Henri Poincaré, *Œuvres*, Tome I (1928) — French text and English translation (partial)

The first part of Volume I of Poincaré's collected works (Paris: Gauthier-Villars, 1928; edited by Paul Appell with
Jules Drach): the French text, typeset in LaTeX from a scan of the print, and an English translation.

## Completeness

This is a partial edition of Tome I. The readers contain, in order:

- the title matter and Appell's preface;
- the first part of Poincaré's *Analyse de ses travaux scientifiques* (on differential equations), with its
  bibliography;
- the note on the properties of functions defined by differential equations;
- the thesis *Sur les propriétés des fonctions définies par les équations aux différences partielles* (1879);
- the *Mémoire sur les courbes définies par une équation différentielle*, with its summary note: Chapters I–XIV and
  XVII–XIX;
- the note *Sur les séries de polynômes*;
- Section I of *Sur les équations linéaires aux différentielles ordinaires et aux différences finies*.

Not yet included: Chapters XV and XVI of the *Mémoire* (a note in the readers marks the place), and the rest of Tome I
from Section II of the last paper on. The 32 figures are reproduced from the scan. The readers do not mark the page
breaks of the print; page references in the text refer to the 1928 volume.

## How it was made

The French text was transcribed from the scan, and translated into English, by AI (OpenAI's ChatGPT), in
installments of about ten pages, each checked against the scan by AI.

## Checks

On 27 October 2026 Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) compared the opening of Chapter XVIII of the *Mémoire* (printed page
189) with the scan; the text and formulas agree with the print. Other pages have not had a second check by a
different model.
